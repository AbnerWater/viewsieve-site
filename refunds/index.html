Subscriptions & cancellation

Subscriptions and billing

Free has no charge. Pro is USD 2.99/month or USD 25.99/year; Max is USD 4.99/month or USD 35.99/year, renewing automatically at the chosen interval. Annual plans are charged once per year and savings compare twelve monthly payments. Review the hosted checkout for applicable taxes and the final total.

Cancellation and refund requests

Use Manage subscription under Plan & usage in the extension to cancel future renewal in the hosted portal. Access continues through the paid period shown by the service. Pausing, uninstalling or disabling configuration sync does not cancel a subscription. Contact support with necessary order details for duplicate charges or other refund requests. No automatic refund, fixed refund window or free trial is promised; statutory rights remain unaffected. Direct plan switching is not currently available in the portal; contact support about changing plans.

Use and support

You can pause or stop using the extension in Chrome. For subscription questions, contact abnerwater@outlook.com. Do not send card details by email.
