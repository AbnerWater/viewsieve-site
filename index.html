FAQ

A few things to know.

Which platforms work today?

The current build supports YouTube in desktop Chrome. Instagram and TikTok are on the roadmap.

What about a video I open myself?

Your actively opened main video stays visible. Surrounding recommendations still follow your rules, and you can show a filtered card once.

Will this remove every ad?

No. Auto-skip clicks a native Skip button once it is available. Unskippable ads, spoken sponsorships and server-stitched ads are not covered by that promise.

Do I need to pay?

The current build is free and retains its existing features. Free / Pro describes future plans; purchasing and trial enrollment are not available.
