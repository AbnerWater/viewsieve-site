FAQ

A few things worth knowing.

Which platforms does ViewSieve support?

YouTube, Instagram and TikTok in desktop Chrome. Each platform has its own content and interface controls. Enable only the platforms you want to use.

What happens after I add a rule?

Matching content disappears immediately. Quick additions offer a 3-second undo. Later matches stay out of view without reason cards. TikTok feeds skip matching items while retaining native navigation.

Will videos I open myself disappear?

Explicitly opened main content has viewing protection. Surrounding recommendations still follow your rules. Entering a generic feed or moving to another item does not grant that protection.

Does auto-skip remove every ad?

No. It uses the native YouTube Skip button when available. Unskippable ads, spoken sponsorships and server-stitched ads are not covered. Dedicated promotional labels are used for content filtering, not brand names or shopping words alone.
