Privacy Policy

Publisher: AbnerWaterEffective date: 2026-09-18

Current extension: processing on your device

After you grant YouTube permission, the extension reads titles, available channel identifiers and other visible fields in supported content areas to evaluate your rules. It does not fetch hidden details to complete a match, inspect private messages or request browsing-history or cookie permissions.

Local configuration

Rules, channel lists, language, module settings, pause state and recovery configuration are stored in Chrome’s local extension storage. The current build has no product account, cloud configuration sync, usage analytics or ViewSieve content-upload service. Settings remain on your device until changed or removed; uninstalling removes extension storage. Browser and operating-system backups are outside ViewSieve’s control.

Automatic ad skipping and permissions

The Enhanced build includes Chrome debugger permission, which has broad technical debugging capability. The implementation uses it for trusted input on a confirmed available native Skip button in the active authorized YouTube watch page. Chrome may display a debugging notice. Filtering also uses storage, scripting and alarms. You may disable auto-skip, pause the extension or revoke YouTube access. This feature does not modify playback rate, volume or main-video progress.

Diagnostics

Player diagnostics expose limited version, mode and failure-stage status locally. They are not automatically uploaded and do not contain rules, video titles or account information. A support report should include only the information you choose to share.

This website

This static site uses GitHub Pages hosting. ViewSieve adds no analytics, advertising pixels, payment SDK, account system, email signup form or non-essential cookies. Fonts and images are served with the site. Demo selections stay in memory and reset on reload. GitHub processes request information such as IP addresses to deliver and protect its service; its own privacy statement governs that processing and retention.

Support contact

Support is provided by AbnerWater at abnerwater@outlook.com using Microsoft Outlook.com. The site has no support form. When you email us, your address, message and attachments are used to respond and resolve the issue. Correspondence is kept only as long as needed for support and applicable legal obligations. Use the same address to request access, correction or deletion; legal retention duties may limit deletion. Microsoft processes email under its privacy statement.

Future services and your choices

Payments, cloud sync and accounts are not active. Their data handling will require an updated notice before introduction. You control local rules and permissions. The current Chrome and YouTube services remain subject to their own policies.
