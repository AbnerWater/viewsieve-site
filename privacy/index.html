Privacy & data

Filtering happens on your device

On authorized supported pages, the extension matches available titles, descriptions, tags and reliable author identifiers. It does not fetch hidden details to complete a match or read private messages or comment bodies. Page content and author lists are not sent to ViewSieve, ExtPay or Stripe.

Local settings and necessary metering

Rules, author lists, platform preferences and recovery snapshots are stored locally. Daily usage, verified subscription cache, granted website permissions, the global switch and pause remain device-local and are excluded from configuration sync. Necessary allowance metering is not usage analytics. No page telemetry or advertising tracking is added.

Optional Chrome configuration sync

Max users may opt in; sync is off by default. After consent, platform settings, layout, keywords and author lists are shared through Chrome and may reveal personal preferences. Chrome and your Google sync settings control delivery. The same account and extension ID are required across devices. The configuration budget is 80 KiB. Payment services do not receive this configuration.

Turning sync off and deleting a copy

Turning sync off stops sharing on this device and retains local and shared copies. Deleting the shared copy requires separate confirmation. Other devices pause once they observe its deletion and retain their local settings. Offline delivery cannot be confirmed here; a device that has not seen the deletion can write old data back. Conflicts require a comparison and choice rather than silent timestamp-based replacement.

Payment login is separate

The extension integrates ExtPay and its Stripe subscription service. During purchase, login recovery and entitlement maintenance, the SDK exchanges account and entitlement information and stores necessary login identifiers; it may use Chrome sync storage. Subscription access is checked at startup, after payment, on manual refresh and hourly. Deleting configuration sync data does not delete payment login data. Rules, author lists, page content and cleanup usage are not sent to payment services.

Permissions and local diagnostics

Storage saves settings, scripting runs on authorized sites, and alarms maintain state. Debugger permission is used to click an available native Skip button on a confirmed active YouTube watch page; Chrome may show a debugging notice. Page diagnostics contain limited runtime status, not rules, page text or account information, and are not automatically uploaded.

Website and contact

GitHub Pages hosts this static website. ViewSieve adds no analytics, ad pixels, payment SDK or product account system. Fonts and images are served locally; demo selections exist only in memory. GitHub processes requests to deliver its service. Support email uses Outlook.com; messages you send are used to address support or privacy requests. Please share only necessary information.
